About this test

Measures the connection between this browser and the server hosting this page. Download and upload use a series of requests of increasing size; the result is the 90th percentile of the measured speeds. Latency is the median time to first byte of empty requests; jitter is the average change between consecutive latency measurements.

Results stay in this browser and are not sent anywhere.

Measurements use the open-source @cloudflare/speedtest engine (MIT licence).
